# Remove a background from an iPhone HEIC photo

HEIC and HEIF are common iPhone image formats. Use the original photo rather than a screenshot when possible.

1. Select a single HEIC or HEIF photo in the [HEIC background remover](https://mattevia.com/heic-background-remover).
2. Check orientation and subject framing in the preview.
3. Remove the background, then inspect the subject against light and dark colors.
4. Choose PNG for a transparent cutout, WebP where your destination supports it, or JPG for a solid background.
5. Download and reopen the result in the destination app.

The result is a still image; this workflow does not preserve Live Photo motion. Large images may be reduced by processing or output-size limits. Check the dimensions shown by the tool rather than assuming original resolution.

If selection fails, check the current file limits and confirm you selected an image. You can export a JPG from your photo application as a fallback, but conversion may change metadata or quality. HEIC compatibility can vary by file variant and device.

The repository examples are PNG-derived demonstration images, not evidence of a native iPhone HEIC device test.
