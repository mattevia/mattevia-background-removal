# Prepare a white-background product photo

Use a clear photo of the complete product. Keep enough margin around laces, straps, handles and other thin parts. Avoid clutter and strong background reflections where possible.

1. Upload the image to the [white background tool](https://mattevia.com/white-background).
2. Inspect the cutout before choosing a background.
3. Select White (#FFFFFF), rather than Transparent.
4. Choose the original canvas or a square canvas as needed.
5. Export JPG, PNG or WebP and check the saved image dimensions.

## Before listing the product

- Check that small parts are still present and holes are properly cleared.
- Inspect pale edges against a darker background before returning to white.
- Verify framing, dimensions, file size and product-content requirements with your marketplace.
- Look for leftover shadows or reflections around the product.

A flat white background does not generate a new studio shadow, correct perspective, or guarantee marketplace approval. Glass and glossy objects may need additional editing.
