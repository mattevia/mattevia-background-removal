The PNG originals were processed through the live Mattevia anonymous upload and background-removal workflow on September 9, 2026. Matching results are actual service outputs without manual retouching or generated substitute masks, according to the project's recorded sample provenance. The WebP files are full-size derivatives for display and download.

These are historical examples, not a new benchmark run. No inference model, processing speed or universal quality guarantee is claimed. The beagle source intentionally has its tail behind its body.

No third-party stock photographs or customer uploads are included in this collection. Filenames and SHA-256 hashes are provided in SHA256SUMS for integrity checking.
